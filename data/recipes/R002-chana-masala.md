# Chana Masala

## Overview

A tangy, entirely plant-based chickpea curry built on a base of onion, tomato and
toasted whole spices. Cooking dried chickpeas from scratch is what separates this
from the tinned-chickpea version: the starchy cooking liquid thickens the sauce and
the chickpeas hold their shape.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dried chickpeas | 200 | g | soaked 8 hours or overnight in cold water |
| Onion | 2 | medium | finely chopped |
| Ripe tomatoes | 400 | g | grated, skins discarded |
| Garlic cloves | 5 | cloves | minced |
| Fresh ginger | 20 | g | julienned |
| Green chillies | 2 | whole | slit lengthways |
| Cumin seeds | 1 | tsp | toasted whole |
| Coriander powder | 2 | tsp | added with the tomato |
| Amchur (dried mango powder) | 1 | tsp | stirred in at the end |
| Turmeric | 0.5 | tsp | |
| Garam masala | 1 | tsp | |
| Neutral oil | 3 | tbsp | |
| Salt | 2 | tsp | to taste |
| Fresh coriander | 1 | handful | to finish |

## Method

1. Drain the soaked chickpeas, cover with fresh water by 5 cm and boil for 45 to 55
   minutes until they crush easily between two fingers. Reserve 250 ml of the
   cooking liquid.
2. Heat the oil and toast the cumin seeds for 30 seconds until they darken.
3. Add the onion and fry for 10 minutes over medium heat until deep brown at the
   edges. This browning is where most of the flavour comes from.
4. Stir in the garlic, ginger and green chillies and cook for 2 minutes.
5. Add the grated tomato, turmeric, coriander powder and salt. Cook for 12 minutes
   until the oil separates and pools at the edges of the pan.
6. Add the drained chickpeas with the reserved cooking liquid and simmer uncovered
   for 15 minutes, mashing a spoonful of chickpeas against the side of the pan to
   thicken the sauce.
7. Finish with the garam masala, amchur and fresh coriander.

## Notes & Substitutions

Tinned chickpeas work in a hurry: use two 400 g tins, drained, and reduce the final
simmer to 8 minutes. The sauce will be thinner because you lose the starchy cooking
water, so mash a few more chickpeas to compensate.

If you have no amchur, one tablespoon of lemon juice added off the heat gives a
comparable sourness. This recipe contains no nuts, dairy or gluten and is suitable
for a strict vegan diet as written.
