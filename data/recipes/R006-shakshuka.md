# Shakshuka

## Overview

Eggs poached directly in a spiced pepper and tomato sauce, cooked and served in the
same pan. A one-pan brunch dish that takes about 35 minutes, most of it spent
reducing the sauce before the eggs go anywhere near it.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large eggs | 6 | eggs | at room temperature |
| Red bell peppers | 2 | whole | sliced into strips |
| Ripe tomatoes | 600 | g | chopped, or one 400 g tin plus 200 g fresh |
| Onion | 1 | large | sliced |
| Garlic cloves | 4 | cloves | sliced |
| Sweet paprika | 2 | tsp | |
| Ground cumin | 1 | tsp | |
| Caraway seeds | 0.5 | tsp | lightly crushed |
| Cayenne pepper | 0.25 | tsp | optional |
| Olive oil | 3 | tbsp | |
| Feta cheese | 100 | g | crumbled over at the end |
| Fresh parsley | 1 | handful | chopped |
| Salt | 1 | tsp | to taste |

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a wide, shallow pan and cook the onion and peppers over
   medium heat for 12 minutes until soft and beginning to colour.
2. Add the garlic, paprika, cumin, caraway and cayenne and fry for 1 minute until
   fragrant.
3. Add the tomatoes and salt, then simmer for 15 minutes, uncovered, until the
   sauce is thick enough to hold a channel when you drag a spoon through it. This
   reduction must happen before the eggs go in; a loose sauce will not support
   them.
4. Make six wells in the sauce with the back of a spoon and crack one egg into
   each.
5. Cover the pan and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, until the whites are set but the
   yolks still move when you shake the pan.
6. Scatter over the crumbled feta and parsley and serve straight from the pan with
   flatbread.

## Notes & Substitutions

For a vegan version, omit the eggs and feta and add two 400 g tins of drained
chickpeas at step 3, simmering for 10 minutes.

The eggs continue cooking in the residual heat of the pan after it leaves the
stove, so pull it a minute earlier than looks right. If your yolks are consistently
overcooked, that residual heat is why.
