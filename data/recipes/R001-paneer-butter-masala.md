# Paneer Butter Masala

## Overview

A rich North Indian curry of pan-seared paneer cubes simmered in a velvety tomato,
butter and cashew gravy. Mildly spiced and slightly sweet, it is the dish most
people order the first time they eat at an Indian restaurant. Total time is about
50 minutes, of which 20 minutes is unattended simmering.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paneer | 400 | g | cut into 2 cm cubes |
| Ripe tomatoes | 600 | g | roughly chopped |
| Raw cashews | 20 | g | soaked in hot water 15 minutes |
| Unsalted butter | 50 | g | divided, half for searing |
| Heavy cream | 60 | ml | stirred in at the end, off the heat |
| Onion | 1 | medium | finely sliced |
| Garlic cloves | 4 | cloves | crushed |
| Fresh ginger | 15 | g | grated |
| Kashmiri chilli powder | 1.5 | tsp | for colour, not heat |
| Garam masala | 1 | tsp | added late |
| Dried fenugreek leaves | 1 | tsp | crushed between the palms |
| Granulated sugar | 1 | tsp | balances the tomato acidity |
| Salt | 1.5 | tsp | to taste |
| Water | 150 | ml | for loosening the gravy |

## Method

1. Soak the cashews in hot water for 15 minutes, then drain.
2. Melt half the butter in a wide pan over medium heat. Sear the paneer cubes for
   2 minutes per side until pale gold, then lift them out and set aside.
3. In the same pan, soften the onion for 8 minutes, then add the garlic and ginger
   and cook for a further 2 minutes.
4. Add the tomatoes, chilli powder, sugar and salt. Cover and cook for 15 minutes
   until the tomatoes collapse completely.
5. Blend the tomato mixture with the drained cashews until entirely smooth, then
   pass it through a sieve back into the pan.
6. Add the water and simmer uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring every few minutes,
   until the gravy coats the back of a spoon.
7. Stir in the remaining butter, the garam masala and the crushed fenugreek leaves.
8. Return the paneer to the pan, warm through for 3 minutes, then take the pan off
   the heat and stir in the heavy cream. Do not boil the curry after adding cream
   or it will split.

## Notes & Substitutions

To make this dish vegan, replace the paneer with the same weight of extra-firm tofu
pressed for 30 minutes, swap the butter for a neutral oil, and use thick coconut
cream in place of the heavy cream. The cashews are structural rather than optional:
they are what gives the gravy its body, so do not omit them without replacing them
with 2 tablespoons of soaked melon seeds.

If the finished gravy tastes sharp, another half teaspoon of sugar will usually fix
it. Leftovers keep for 3 days refrigerated but the cream may separate on reheating.
