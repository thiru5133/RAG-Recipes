# Vegan Thai Green Curry with Tofu

## Overview

The plant-based version of the classic green curry. Pressed tofu is seared before
it goes into the sauce so it holds together, and light soy sauce plus a little
white miso replaces the savoury depth that fish sauce would normally provide.
Ready in 40 minutes including pressing time.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extra-firm tofu | 400 | g | pressed 30 minutes, cubed |
| Green curry paste | 50 | g | check the label, many brands contain shrimp |
| Coconut milk | 400 | ml | full fat, do not shake the tin |
| Coconut cream | 100 | ml | skimmed from the top of the tin |
| Light soy sauce | 2 | tbsp | replaces fish sauce |
| White miso paste | 1 | tsp | for savoury depth |
| Palm sugar | 1.5 | tbsp | shaved |
| Thai aubergines | 150 | g | quartered |
| Bamboo shoots | 100 | g | drained |
| Makrut lime leaves | 4 | leaves | torn, stems removed |
| Thai basil | 1 | handful | added off the heat |
| Vegetable stock | 100 | ml | |
| Neutral oil | 2 | tbsp | for searing the tofu |

## Method

1. Press the tofu between two plates under a weight for 30 minutes, then cut it
   into 2.5 cm cubes.
2. Sear the tofu in the neutral oil over high heat until golden on at least three
   sides, then lift it out and reserve.
3. Boil the thick coconut cream hard in the same pan for 3 to 4 minutes until it
   splits and the oil separates.
4. Fry the green curry paste in the split cream for 3 minutes until it darkens.
   Whisk in the white miso.
5. Add the remaining coconut milk and the vegetable stock, bring to a bare simmer,
   then add the aubergines, bamboo shoots and lime leaves.
6. Simmer for 10 minutes, then return the seared tofu and simmer for 5 minutes more
   so it absorbs the sauce without falling apart.
7. Season with the soy sauce and palm sugar, take off the heat and stir through the
   Thai basil.

## Notes & Substitutions

Read the curry paste label carefully. Most supermarket green curry pastes contain
shrimp paste or fish sauce and are not vegan despite the green vegetable imagery on
the tub. Vegan-certified brands exist, or you can blend your own from green
chillies, lemongrass, galangal, coriander root, cumin and white pepper.

Tempeh works in place of tofu and needs no pressing, though the flavour is nuttier.
This version is free of dairy, gluten and nuts as written.
