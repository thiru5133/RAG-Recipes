# Thai Green Curry with Chicken

## Overview

A fast weeknight curry: the coconut cream is split first to release its oil, the
paste is fried in that oil, and the chicken poaches gently in the finished sauce.
Ready in 35 minutes. Seasoned with fish sauce, so it is not vegetarian.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Unit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chicken thigh | 500 | g | boneless, sliced 1 cm thick |
| Green curry paste | 45 | g | about 3 tablespoons |
| Coconut milk | 400 | ml | full fat, do not shake the tin |
| Coconut cream | 100 | ml | skimmed from the top of the tin |
| Fish sauce | 2 | tbsp | the primary salt source |
| Palm sugar | 1.5 | tbsp | shaved |
| Thai aubergines | 150 | g | quartered |
| Bamboo shoots | 100 | g | drained |
| Makrut lime leaves | 4 | leaves | torn, stems removed |
| Thai basil | 1 | handful | added off the heat |
| Red chilli | 1 | whole | sliced, for garnish |
| Chicken stock | 100 | ml | or water |

## Method

1. Spoon the thick coconut cream from the top of the tin into a hot wok and boil it
   hard for 3 to 4 minutes until it splits and the oil separates.
2. Add the green curry paste to the split cream and fry for 3 minutes until it
   darkens and smells toasted rather than raw.
3. Add the chicken and turn it in the paste for 2 minutes to coat every piece.
4. Pour in the remaining coconut milk and the stock, bring to a bare simmer and add
   the aubergines, bamboo shoots and lime leaves.
5. Simmer gently for 12 minutes. Do not let it boil hard or the chicken toughens
   and the coconut milk splits.
6. Season with the fish sauce and palm sugar, then taste and adjust: the balance
   should be salty first, sweet second, hot third.
7. Take off the heat, stir through the Thai basil and scatter over the sliced
   chilli.

## Notes & Substitutions

Chicken breast can replace thigh but reduce the simmer to 7 minutes or it will dry
out. Regular aubergine cut into 2 cm cubes is a fine substitute for Thai aubergines.

The heat of commercial green curry pastes varies enormously between brands. Start
with 30 g if you are using an unfamiliar paste and add more after tasting at step
6. This dish contains fish sauce and is therefore not suitable for vegetarians or
vegans; see the tofu version for a plant-based alternative.
